Customer Portal Release Report
Reporting Period: Q2 2025

Overview

During the second quarter of 2025, the company released an updated customer self-service portal across several European markets.

The release introduced improvements to account management, invoice viewing, support case tracking, and service administration.

Rollout

The updated portal was gradually released to customers in Germany, France, Spain, and the Netherlands.

Enterprise customers received additional account-management features designed for organizations with multiple users and services.

Customer Experience

Most customers successfully transitioned to the updated portal.

Some users reported difficulty locating previously available account settings after the interface redesign.

Support teams published additional guidance and updated help documentation to assist customers with the new navigation.

Billing Features

Customers could continue viewing invoices and payment history through the portal.

The release did not change the underlying systems responsible for processing customer payments.

Next Steps

Product teams will continue collecting customer feedback and improving portal usability during future releases.
