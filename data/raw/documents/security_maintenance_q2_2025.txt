Security Maintenance Report
Reporting Period: Q2 2025

Overview

During the second quarter of 2025, security engineering teams completed scheduled maintenance across several internal and customer-facing systems.

Maintenance Activities

Teams applied operating system updates, rotated service credentials, and deployed security patches addressing recently identified software vulnerabilities.

Maintenance was performed across infrastructure supporting customers in Germany, France, Spain, and the Netherlands.

Customer Impact

Most maintenance activities were completed without customer-facing disruption.

A small number of Enterprise customers experienced brief authentication interruptions during scheduled maintenance windows.

No significant loss of customer data or prolonged service outage was identified during the maintenance period.

Security Monitoring

Security teams increased monitoring during and after each maintenance window.

Automated security controls continued detecting unusual authentication activity and potential configuration issues.

Next Steps

Engineering teams will continue scheduled patching, vulnerability assessment, and security monitoring throughout the remainder of 2025.
