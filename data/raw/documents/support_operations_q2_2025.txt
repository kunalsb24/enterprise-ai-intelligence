Support Operations Review
Reporting Period: Q2 2025

Overview

Customer support teams handled increased case volumes during parts of the second quarter of 2025 across several European markets.

Ticket Trends

Billing-related support requests increased during April and May.

Support teams received questions about invoices, unsuccessful payment attempts, account balances, and payment retries.

The increase was particularly noticeable among Enterprise customers in Germany.

Other support categories, including technical assistance and account administration, remained closer to normal operating levels.

Customer Experience

Some customers contacted support multiple times while attempting to resolve billing and payment-related issues.

Support agents provided payment retry guidance and escalated unresolved cases to billing operations when necessary.

Operations Response

Additional support capacity was assigned to billing queues during periods of elevated demand.

Support managers also improved escalation procedures between customer support and billing operations.

By the end of June, billing-related ticket volumes had begun moving back toward normal levels.
